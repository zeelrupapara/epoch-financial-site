## Three findings

**1. Set B does not compete with EPOCH in search.** Zero of the ten crawled firms
mention accounts receivable financing or asset-based lending. They sell to LPs and
PE sponsors, not to borrowers typing queries into Google. Their sites are
institutional brochures: 150–2,000 words, no FAQ, no service pages, no geo pages.
They rank for their own brand names. They are not a ranking threat — and that is
the opportunity, not a problem.

**2. Every one of them leads with hard numbers. EPOCH does not.** Golub opens with
$90B AUM. Antares publishes a 0.07% loss rate. Audax states senior debt up to $200M.
EPOCH's homepage shows facility sizes only, and only below the fold — no advance
rate, no pricing, no funding speed. EPOCH has better *borrower-relevant* numbers
than any of them (85–90% advance rates, 0.75–1.50%/month, 24–48 hour funding,
$3M–$200M+ facilities) and currently hides them. `SEO_MASTER_PLAN.md` §6.2 already
calls this out: these are the rare, citable facts AI answers lift.

**3. Nobody in the tier writes borrower-facing content.** None of the ten has an
FAQ. None has a service page explaining mechanics. The middle-market keyword layer
— "middle market asset-based lending", "non-bank ABL lender", "borrowing base
financing" — is held by PDFs and glossary stubs. EPOCH already has 140 URLs, FAQ
sections on 22 pages, and a schema pipeline none of them run.

## What this changes

Benchmark **rankings** against Set A. Benchmark **positioning and proof** against
Set B. The near-term SEO work does not change: own the middle-market tier that
Set B ignores and Set A cannot reach.

The one immediate action this teardown adds: **put EPOCH's numbers on the first
screen of the homepage and every service page.** That is the single thing every
Set B firm does better, and EPOCH has stronger numbers to show.

## Not measured

Search rankings. Positions require a paid SERP API (DataForSEO, Bright Data or
similar) — none is configured. This teardown is content and positioning only.
