### Who EPOCH Financial is

EPOCH Financial is a **commercial finance advisory firm** headquartered in
Atlanta, Georgia, focused on structured credit solutions for middle market
companies. Since 1999, EPOCH has supported middle market businesses,
management teams, and private equity sponsors. EPOCH **structures and
facilitates** senior secured credit solutions — it is an advisor and
facilitator between borrowers and institutional lenders, **not the direct
lender**. Transactions are funded by banks, private credit funds, and
specialty finance groups that EPOCH coordinates.

The platform is built around disciplined working capital strategies where
**accounts receivable is the anchor collateral** — AR financing is the #1
product focus, with asset-based lending, bridge capital, and strategic
capital solutions built around it.
